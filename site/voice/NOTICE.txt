LOCAL VOICE – licences and attribution (chitas-reader, site/voice/)
===================================================================
These files are downloaded to the reader's device only when the "קול איכותי" (quality voice) button is pressed.
Speech is synthesised on the device; no text is sent anywhere.

1. he_IL-noa-medium.onnx, he_IL-noa-medium.onnx.json
   "Piper Hebrew – noa (medium)" by thewh1teagle – https://huggingface.co/thewh1teagle/piper-he-noa
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0) – https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
   Unmodified copy (sha256 of the .onnx identical to the Hugging Face file).

2. he_IL-noa-medium-int8.onnx
   Adapted from (1): weights quantised to int8 (ONNX Runtime dynamic quantisation) by the chitas-reader project, for
   weak devices. This is a modified version of the original. Licence: CC BY 4.0, as (1). No endorsement by the original
   author is implied.

3. ort/ort.wasm.bundle.min.mjs, ort/ort-wasm-simd-threaded.wasm
   ONNX Runtime Web 1.31.1 – Copyright (c) Microsoft Corporation – MIT License (ort/LICENSE).
   The WebAssembly binary includes third-party components; their notices: ort/ThirdPartyNotices.txt
   (from https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/blob/v1.31.1/ThirdPartyNotices.txt).

4. Hebrew grapheme-to-phoneme rules (site/js/g2p.js) – JavaScript port of the rules of Phonikud 0.4.1
   by thewh1teagle – https://github.com/thewh1teagle/phonikud – CC BY 4.0. Changed: ported to JavaScript; added stress
   from cantillation marks, a stress dictionary/suffix model, vocal-shva and qamatz-katan rules (chitas-reader).
   The phoneme convention follows renikud (https://renikud.github.io), which the voice was trained on.

5. stress-he.json – built by chitas-reader (tools/build-voice-stress.mjs) from the cantillation marks of the Torah and
   Tehillim texts in data/ (see the site's Sources page) and a hand-checked exception list. Part of the site's code
   (GPL-2.0-or-later).

6. Male timbre (site/js/voice-pitch.js, since 1.0.60) – own code of chitas-reader (GPL-2.0-or-later), no third-party
   DSP library: YIN pitch estimation (de Cheveigné & Kawahara, 2002 – method only), TD-PSOLA pitch shift (Moulines &
   Charpentier, 1990 – method only) and windowed-sinc resampling. It changes the audio produced by (1)/(2) on the
   device: pitch about 6 semitones lower, formants about 6% lower. The result is a modified rendering of the noa
   voice, not a recording or model of a male speaker; no endorsement by the original author is implied.
   No other voice model is downloaded for it. Male Hebrew voices that were checked and NOT used (licence of the
   weights or of the training recordings is non-commercial or undocumented): see verification/VOICE.md.

No code of Piper (GPL) is used: the model is run with ONNX Runtime Web and the input is prepared by g2p.js/piper-core.js.

7. he_IL-ilspeech-male-medium.onnx, he_IL-ilspeech-male-medium.onnx.json (since 1.0.88; downloaded only when the
   reader chooses "קול גברי טבעי")
   "piper_medium_male.onnx" and "model.config.json" from https://huggingface.co/notmax123/piper-medium-heb
   (revision 159c04c325df208e2045d929f11a25015942729a) by notmax123.
   Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0) –
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/ . Trained on the ILSpeech recordings of the Phonikud project
   (CC BY-NC 4.0). Use here is non-commercial (a free app with no ads or payments).
   The .onnx is an unmodified copy (sha256 9971617aaa8786aea27325ac458fc4371d54af0d4316c2002b76789806cb2090), renamed.
   The .onnx.json is the original model.config.json with one added field, "chitas_pad": false (the model takes phoneme
   ids without the '_' pad between symbols). No endorsement by the original author is implied.

8. ../voice-samples/*.mp3 – short preview recordings of the four voice choices, synthesised by chitas-reader with the
   models above (male, deep, female: noa, CC BY 4.0, our signal processing; natural: the model of (7), CC BY-NC 4.0).
